# Surface brief: whole site redesign

Mode: Persuade. Visitor decides to message FAUVE on WhatsApp. User pinned the direction: dark-warm editorial, Byredo-like full-bleed photography with giant wordmark over image; images are tawny placeholders for now (user choice).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A night-lit gallery of tawny stone and amber light. One full-bleed image per section, almost no UI chrome, big quiet type. Refuses the split-60/40 template, eyebrows, boxed cards and the floating product on blank beige.

OWN-WORLD: Warm near-black umber ground (#16110E), bone text, sand (#E9DDC6) and stone (#E5E3DB) bands for contrast, one drenched terracotta (#A05034) closing field, olive hairlines. Inter 300 at large sizes, uppercase 11-12px tracked labels. Placeholder plates are layered tawny gradients (canyon, driftwood, stone, dune, amber, grove) with grain. Rectangular, no radius, hairline borders.

STORY: Visitor feels the brand in one viewport, understands fragrance house plus perfume bar plus manufacturing from three stacked panels, and finishes at a terracotta WhatsApp call to action.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 100svh plate, giant white FAUVE wordmark centred in the upper third at about 60vw, small menu word left and "Find your scent" right in the top bar, tagline "Fragrance tells your story." bottom-left, outlined CTA bottom-right.

FORM: Byredo-style hero plus Diptyque-style stacked full-bleed panels (sticky, each slides over the last) as the signature scroll moment; seed: none (unavailable), user-pinned direction.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
