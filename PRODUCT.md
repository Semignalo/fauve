# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Next.js 16 App Router + TypeScript (existing, user-approved). English only.

## Users
Hosts and planners of weddings, brand activations and private evenings who want a bespoke perfume bar; brands and founders who want a fragrance developed and manufactured; individuals exploring a scent for themselves. They arrive on phone first, often from Instagram or WhatsApp referrals, and act by messaging on WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose
FAUVE is a fragrance house and manufacturing partner. It runs bespoke perfume-bar pop-ups and develops fragrance for brands. The site must make a visitor feel the brand, show packages and full pricing, and move them to a WhatsApp enquiry. Success: qualified WhatsApp enquiries for Perfume Bar and Manufacturing.

## Positioning
"Fragrance tells your story." Scent as a personal story, made with the guest in the room or built from a brand's brief. Vision: to make the meaningful linger through scent.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Pages: Home, About, Perfume Bar, Manufacturing, Find your scent (multiple-choice quiz, rule-based matching, results are information only), Contact.
- CTA: WhatsApp button and an inquiry form that opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message.
- Never mention AI, algorithms or automation anywhere.
- Copy voice: calm, story-first, short declarative sentences, no exclamation marks, no hype.
- Still empty and owned by the user: scent database (inputs/1-scents.csv), packages and prices (data/packages.ts), contact details (data/contact.ts). Do not fabricate them.

## Brand Commitments
Name FAUVE /fohv/ (French: tawny tones of earth, wood, light, animal fur). Thin, widely spaced uppercase wordmark (assets in public/img/logo). Palette from the brand guideline: #E5E3DB, #E9DDC6, #A05034 terracotta, #747B4D olive, #1D1D1D. Fonts named in guideline: Inter, Helvetica Neue, Rotis Sans Serif. Moodboard: shells, stacked stones, driftwood, dried plants, fig, B&W hands, canyon light.

## Evidence on Hand
Logo files, 30 ml and 50 ml bottle mockups, a 3D bottle render, ingredient stock images (inconsistent in colour; being retired). No event or product photography exists yet. Image slots use tawny placeholders until real photography arrives.

## Product Principles
1. Image-led and quiet: one photograph carries each section.
2. Story first, product second.
3. Everything swappable: photography, prices and contact data arrive later.
4. WhatsApp is the destination; never hide it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast, visible focus, full keyboard use of the Scent Finder, reduced-motion respected.
